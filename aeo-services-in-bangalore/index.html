AEO vs SEO

What AEO is and how it differs from SEO

AEO is the work of getting your brand named and cited inside AI-generated answers. SEO still matters: it earns rankings and clicks on a results page. AEO covers the step before the click, when an engine writes a summary after reading several sources. If your page gives a direct answer with proof behind it, the engine can cite it. If the page is vague, the engine cites a competitor.

AreaSEOAEO
GoalRank pages and earn clicksGet cited and named in answers
Where it showsSearch results pagesAI answers, summaries and chat replies
Core inputsKeywords, links, page speedDirect answers, entity clarity, structured data, third-party mentions
Main measuresRankings and organic trafficCitations, mentions and share of answer

Good AEO sits on top of good SEO. A site with crawl errors or thin pages won't get cited, so we fix the basics first when they're broken.

Process

How our AEO process works

01

Audit

We run a fixed set of prompts across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews, then record who gets cited and how your brand is described. We also crawl your site for technical blockers. You get a plain-language report, usually within 2 weeks.

02

Prompt map

We turn the audit and your sales notes into a ranked list of questions worth winning. Each question gets an owner page, a target answer and a proof point to back it up.

03

Foundation fixes

We repair crawl access, rendering, schema and entity data so machines can read your pages. This step is quick for many sites.

04

Content

We write new answer-first pages and rework the ones that already earn traffic. Every page opens with the direct answer, then adds detail, data and sources.

05

Citations

We earn mentions on the sources engines read, through outreach, digital PR and accurate listings. Mentions only count when they're real, so we don't buy placements.

06

Review

Each month we rerun the prompt set, compare results to the baseline and adjust the plan.

Steps 3 to 5 overlap in practice. Fixes, content and citation work run in parallel, and each month's review decides where the next hours go.
