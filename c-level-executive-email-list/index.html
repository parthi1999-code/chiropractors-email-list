Compliance

Compliance for US and International Campaigns

The list is built for lawful B2B outreach, and each file ships with the suppression data you need to stay compliant.

In the US, the CAN-SPAM Act governs commercial email and makes no exception for B2B messages. The Act doesn't require prior consent, and penalties reach up to $53,088 for each email that breaks its rules. Every message must meet the requirements set out in the FTC compliance guide:

Accurate sender details and subject lines that match the content
A clear and visible disclosure that the message is an ad
A valid physical postal address for your business
A working opt-out, with requests honored within 10 business days
EU and UK

In the EU and UK, GDPR governs the personal data, and national ePrivacy laws set the email rules, which differ by country. The UK lets you email corporate subscribers, such as limited companies and LLPs, without prior consent, while Germany requires consent even for B2B email. Sole traders and some partnerships count as individual subscribers in the UK, so emailing the founders of those firms needs consent.

Canada

Canada's anti-spam law, CASL, requires express or implied consent. A published business email supports implied consent only when your message relates to the executive's role and the address carries no statement refusing marketing. Canadian records can also be filtered out of any file.
