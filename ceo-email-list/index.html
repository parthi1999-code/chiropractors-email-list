Compliance

Built for Compliant Outreach

The rules for emailing CEOs depend on where each one is based. The US CAN-SPAM Act allows B2B email that includes a postal address and a working opt-out, and each email that breaks the rules can draw a penalty of up to US$53,088. The FTC also expects honest headers and subject lines, plus opt-outs honored within 10 business days. Since January 1, 2023, California's CCPA has also given business contacts rights such as deletion, for companies that meet its thresholds.

Canada's CASL requires consent, and its published-address rule doesn't cover addresses scraped from third-party directories. The UK lets you email company addresses without prior consent under PECR, though sole traders and some partnerships need it, and the EU expects a lawful basis such as legitimate interests. Each record carries its headquarters country, so you can apply the right rules to each segment before you send.
