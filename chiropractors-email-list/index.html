How Chiropractic Clinics Are Set Up and Paid

Most chiropractors work in small clinics they own or share with a few partners, so buying decisions usually sit with a clinic owner. Our chiropractors email database tags each practice by setup and payment model, which lets a vendor size its market before the first send goes out.

Solo Owner Clinic: 1 DC handles patient care and the business, so the owner signs every purchase.

Multi-DC Group: Several DCs share 1 or more locations, with an owner or partner approving spend.

Multidisciplinary Clinic: DCs work alongside physical therapists or medical doctors in 1 shared clinic.

Franchise Location: A local owner runs a branded clinic under a national franchise system's rules.

Payment model matters because Medicare Part B covers a chiropractor's manual manipulation of the spine, so chiropractor mailing lists should keep Medicare-billing clinics apart from cash-based ones. Specialty is tagged too, using NPI chiropractic codes, so a sports or pediatric campaign can start narrow before widening to the full chiropractors email list.

Why Our Chiropractors Email List Holds Licensed DCs Only

The core email list of chiropractors holds licensed Doctors of Chiropractic only, each matched to a state license and an active clinic. That covers solo practitioners, clinic owners and partners who approve purchases, salaried associates and DCs at multidisciplinary or franchise locations.

Students, college faculty, sales reps and title-only contacts sit in separate segments, so chiropractor email lists built this way hold no title matches.
