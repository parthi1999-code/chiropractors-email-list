A CIO mailing list gives your sales and marketing teams a direct line to the executives who run IT. CIOs set the technology roadmap and own the budget that pays for new vendors. Reaching them early can put your product on the shortlist before anyone writes an RFP.

That budget is growing fast. Gartner forecasts worldwide IT spending of $7.35 trillion in 2027, up 15% from 2026 (Gartner, October 2026). About 51% of the increase comes from data center systems, as companies build capacity for AI workloads.

The CIO's remit keeps widening too. Many CIOs now own data and security programs on top of core infrastructure, so more vendor categories end up on the same desk. Even when a deal starts with an IT director or an architect, it usually needs the CIO's sign-off before a contract goes out.

The Infinyx Global CIO email database covers these leaders across industries and regions. Each record links to a company profile you can filter by headcount or revenue, so you reach the accounts that fit your offer.

Most buyers sell to IT departments, including cloud and SaaS vendors, cybersecurity firms, IT consultancies, managed service providers and hardware makers. If CIOs approve what you sell, this list gives you their work email.

Advantages

Advantages of Owning a Chief Information Officer Email List

01
Straight to the decision-maker

A Chief Information Officer email list gets your message to the person who signs off on IT purchases. Cold calls rarely make it past an assistant, and generic inboxes like info@ seldom reach the executive at all. A verified work email puts your pitch in front of the decision maker directly.

02
Precise targeting

The list also lets you target with precision. You can segment by industry or company size and write to each group about the problems it actually has. A hospital CIO worries about patient data rules, while a bank CIO answers to financial regulators, and your message can reflect that difference.

03
New markets without new lists

Regional coverage helps you move into new markets without building a list from scratch. You can start with a single country and expand once you see which segments respond.

04
Works across channels

The same data works across other channels too. Teams use their CIO mailing lists for account-based marketing, webinar invites, direct mail, sales calls and LinkedIn ad audiences. Many see a better response when they combine 2 channels, such as an email followed by a call a few days later.

05
Time back for your reps

Owning the data also saves time. Your reps stop hunting for contact details by hand and spend those hours on conversations. Over a quarter, that adds up to a lot more outreach from the same team.

06
Easy to measure

Results are easy to measure, too. You can track replies and meetings booked for each segment, then move budget toward the segments that work.

07
Keeps working after launch

A list you own keeps working long after your launch campaign. You can come back to the same segments with new messages as your product and market change.
