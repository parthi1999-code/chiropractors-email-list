The basics

What's a CRM Users Email List?

CRM stands for customer relationship management, the software that sales and service teams use to track accounts and deals. A CRM users email list is a set of verified business contacts at companies that run a specific CRM platform. Knowing the platform tells you what an account can integrate with and where its sales process might be stuck. It also hints at how mature the sales setup is, since a company on Siebel works very differently from one on HubSpot.

Each contact in our CRM technology users email list comes tagged with the platform their company uses. A record might show that a 250-person software firm runs HubSpot. Its head of sales operations and a verified email sit in the same row. That pairing lets you write to the person who owns the CRM day to day.

Who Uses a CRM Users Contact List?

A CRM users contact list fits any company that sells to teams already working in a CRM. Here's how 8 types of buyer put it to work.

CRM vendors

Find companies on a rival CRM and make the case for switching. Sales and operations leaders who feel the limits of their current tool are the right first contact.

Marketing automation vendors

B2B marketing agencies

IT services and managed service providers

Offer CRM migration or support to companies on older platforms such as Siebel. Their IT teams often need extra hands to keep those systems running.

How we build it

How We Build and Maintain Our CRM Users Database

Each record passes 5 checks before it lands in your file. Every check removes a specific kind of bad record before you ever see it.

01

Confirm the CRM in use

We record which CRM each company runs and, where we can confirm it, the edition. Those platform records feed all 460+ lists in the database. Older versions are noted so legacy users can be pulled separately.

02

Attach the firmographics

Each platform record links to industry, employee count, revenue and location data. You can then filter on CRM and company fit in the same pass. A 50-person Pipedrive shop and a 5,000-person Salesforce org sit in separate segments.

03

Add named contacts

We add the leaders and admins who own the CRM, with job titles attached. Titles help you separate budget holders from the people who use it daily. Contacts without a confirmed title are left out.

04

Check every email

Each address is verified before it joins a list, along with the record around it. Failed records are pulled for rework and don't reach you. Generic inboxes such as info@ are flagged, so you can keep or drop them.

05

Refresh every 90 days

The full database is refreshed every 90 days to catch CRM switches and job moves. Your list stays in step with the tools companies use now. Each record carries the date it was last checked.

Accuracy and compliance

CRM Data Accuracy and Sending Compliance

Data accuracy holds at 95% across our CRM lists. Hard bounces get replaced with fresh records within 30 days, and files reach you over encrypted SFTP. Every sample row shows where the record came from and when it was last verified. Each file is also checked against the filters you approved before it ships.

Your sending practices decide compliance. Under the GDPR, EU contacts need a lawful basis before you email them. The CCPA gives California residents the right to have their data deleted. The CAN-SPAM Act also covers US business email. Each campaign should do these 4 things:

Include a real physical postal address in each message.
Give every reader an opt-out and honor it within 10 business days.
Keep sender details accurate and subject lines true to the content.
Label the email clearly and visibly as an advertisement.
