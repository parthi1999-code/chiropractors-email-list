What global B2B data includes
Global B2B data is verified information about companies and the people who buy for them, collected across countries and kept current. Infinyx Global delivers it in 3 layers inside 1 file, so the global B2B data is ready for segmentation on day 1 of the project.
Company layer. Legal name, website, country, industry, employee size and revenue band define each firm, and together they describe an ideal customer profile that a sales team can filter on.
Contact layer. Name, job title, seniority, verified work email and, where available, a direct phone number identify each decision-maker, so outreach reaches a buyer instead of a general inbox.
Context layer. Technology in use, parent company and headquarters or branch status help a team rank accounts before the first call, which saves time on firms that don't fit.

	How Infinyx Global verifies global B2B data
Every record in Infinyx Global's global B2B data passes 6 checks before delivery, and the same checks apply to vertical files such as the healthcare email lists:
1. Source: records come from company registries, company websites, professional profiles and licensed partner feeds.
2. Match: each company and each contact is confirmed against a second source before the 2 are linked.
3. Title check: job titles and seniority levels are confirmed, so a "head of IT" isn't filed as an intern.
4. Mailbox test: each email address is validated, and role or shared inboxes are flagged for review.
5. Cleanup: duplicates are merged and opted-out contacts are removed before the file is exported.
6. Quarterly refresh: the file is rechecked every quarter, and a failed check sends the record back for correction.
Data profiling and validation services
Infinyx Global treats profiling and validation as 2 separate jobs, and a global B2B data file needs both before it can be trusted.
