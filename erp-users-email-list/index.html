The basics

What's an ERP Users Email List?

ERP stands for enterprise resource planning, the software that runs a company's finance and operations. An ERP users email list is a set of verified business contacts at companies that run a specific ERP system. Common examples include SAP, Oracle, Microsoft Dynamics 365 and NetSuite. Knowing which ERP an account runs tells you what it can integrate with and which problems its team is likely to face.

Each contact in our ERP users email database comes tagged with the platform their company runs. A record might show that a 600-person distributor runs NetSuite, with its controller's verified email and phone number in the same row. That pairing lets you write to the people who work inside the system every day.

How We Build and Maintain Our ERP Users Email Database

Each record goes through 5 checks before it lands in your file. Every check targets a different failure, from a wrong platform tag to a contact who has left the company.

01

Confirm the ERP platform

We record which ERP each company runs, along with the modules in use where we can confirm them. Those platform records form the base of all 390+ lists.

02

Link the company profile

Every platform record connects to the company's industry, employee count, revenue and location. That lets a list filter by company fit and by ERP together.

03

We attach named contacts with job titles for the people who own and run the ERP. Titles let you separate budget holders from the admins who manage it daily.

04

Our team checks each record and email address before it joins a list. Any record that fails the check is held back from your file until it passes.

05

We refresh the full database every 90 days to catch ERP migrations and job changes. Each list then reflects the platforms companies run today.

Accuracy and compliance

ERP Data Accuracy and Sending Compliance

Every ERP record is verified before it reaches you, and data accuracy holds at 95%. Hard bounces are replaced with fresh records within 30 days. Files move to you over encrypted SFTP, and each sample shows the source and last verification date for every row.

Compliance depends on how you send. EU contacts fall under the GDPR, so confirm your lawful basis before the first email. California residents can ask you to delete their data under the CCPA. The CAN-SPAM Act applies to US business email as well. Check that each campaign does these 4 things:

Show a valid physical postal address in every email you send.
Offer a clear opt-out and act on each request within 10 business days.
Use accurate sender details and subject lines that match the content.
State clearly and visibly that the message is an advertisement.
