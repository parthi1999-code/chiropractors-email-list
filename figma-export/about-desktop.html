Who we are

Growth should not need a patchwork of vendors

Infinyx Global is a new-generation B2B growth company, established in 2022. One agency for data, another for outreach, a third for SEO and a fourth for hiring means slow handoffs and no shared accountability.

We built Infinyx to remove that friction. Our developers, designers, testers, data specialists, demand-generation marketers, search strategists, recruiters and business development executives work to the same plan, against the same targets.

Whether you need a clean contact list, a pipeline of qualified meetings, visibility in Google and AI answers, or a sharp technical hire from India, you get one partner and one point of accountability.
