FAQ

Chiropractors Email List: Straight Answers Before You Buy

Which teams use a chiropractors email list most often?

It's built for vendors and recruiters who need to reach licensed Doctors of Chiropractic where they work. Each DC in the file is verified and tied to an active clinic, reachable by email or by post.

Who is left out of the core email list of chiropractors?

Students and vendors who only mention chiropractic in a job title sit in separate segments. The core file holds licensed DCs at active clinics, so every contact can buy from you.

How big is the chiropractors email database?

Infinyx Global currently maintains 230K+ verified chiropractic contacts. Your exact number depends on the specialty and state filters you pick, and we confirm it before you place an order.

Which targeting filters are available in chiropractor email lists?

Start with general chiropractic or any of the 11 NPI chiropractic specializations, then narrow by state, ZIP code, practice setup or payment model. You could order only sports DCs in Colorado.

What does the practice model tag tell me?

It shows how the clinic is set up, whether solo, multi-DC, multidisciplinary or franchise. It also shows whether the clinic bills insurance and Medicare or runs mostly on cash payments.

Can I reach chiropractors who bill Medicare?

Yes. Records carry a billing flag, which helps coding and documentation vendors focus on the clinics that file Medicare Part B claims for manual manipulation of the spine.

Does the chiropractor email list include associate DCs or only owners?

Both. Owners and partner DCs carry an ownership tag, and associates are listed under the clinic where they practice, so you can pull either group on its own for a campaign or a hiring drive.

Is consent needed before emailing chiropractors at their clinics?

No, not in the US. Each message still needs a truthful sender and subject line, an ad disclosure, your postal address and a working unsubscribe link, and contacts abroad may fall under GDPR or CASL.

Is a chiropractors mailing list covered by HIPAA?

No. HIPAA's privacy rule protects patient records held by providers and health plans, and this file lists clinic business contacts only, so nothing in it counts as protected health information.

How does the CCPA affect emailing chiropractors in California?

The CCPA now gives California business contacts the right to request deletion. If your company is covered by the law, a California DC can ask you to delete their details, and you'll need to act on it.

Can I use your chiropractor mailing lists for postcards and catalogs?

Yes. Clinic street addresses are run through address standardization before delivery, so the same file handles your email sends and your printed catalogs without a second data purchase or cleanup.

When do chiropractor records get rechecked?

A record goes back through every check once each review cycle comes round. Retired DCs and closed clinics drop out, and newly licensed DCs join with their clinic links confirmed against current license records.

How is my quote calculated?

Your quote is built from the brief you send and confirmed after your sample test, so it matches the exact file you'll receive from us, with no later changes.

What format do files arrive in, and how fast?

Files come as CSV or Excel within a few business days of sample approval. Headers never change from one order to the next, so a repeat import needs no new mapping.

How do I get a chiropractor to reply to a cold email?

Lead with a specific clinic problem, such as claim denials, and keep the email short. Measure replies and booked calls, because Apple's Mail Privacy Protection preloads messages and skews open rates.

What happens if an email bounces?

Each email is tested to a 97% deliverability standard before delivery. A hard bounce within the replacement window gets replaced with a newly verified record from the same specialty and state you ordered.
