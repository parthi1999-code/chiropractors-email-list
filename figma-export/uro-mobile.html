FAQ

Buyer Questions About the Urologist Email List

Who and what does a urologist email list cover?

Each row ties a licensed urologist to a verified work email at a named practice, along with a primary subspecialty tag. It gives device sellers and urology recruiters a direct line to the doctors they need.

Are nurses or practice staff mixed into the urologists email lists?

No. The core file holds licensed urologists with an MD or DO degree, and urology nurses or administrators sit in separate lists that you can add to the same order on request.

What is the total size of the urologist email database?

The file holds 62K+ verified urology contacts from Infinyx Global. Filtering by subspecialty and state narrows that figure, and we share the matched count before any order is placed.

Which filters can I apply to urologist email addresses?

Pick general urology or a subspecialty such as endourology or urologic oncology, then narrow by state, ZIP code, practice setting or surgical focus. A file of only pediatric urologists in Ohio is possible.

What does the primary subspecialty tag mean?

It marks the 1 area that leads a urologist's practice, so segment counts don't overlap. Secondary tags record other areas they treat, which you can include or leave out of a file.

Can I reach urologists in independent groups only?

Yes. Records carry a practice setting tag, so you can pull partners in independent urology groups without adding hospital-employed or academic urologists to the same file.

Can I target urologists who perform robotic surgery?

Yes. Robotic and laparoscopic surgery is one of the tagged practice areas, and surgical focus tags separate frequent operators from urologists who run office-based practices.

Do I need consent to email urologists at their practices?

Not in the US, where CAN-SPAM allows B2B email if each message carries a truthful sender and subject line, an ad disclosure, your postal address and a working unsubscribe link.

Does HIPAA cover a urologist mailing list?

No. The privacy rule covers patient records that providers and health plans hold. This urology file carries business contact details for practices, so there's no protected health information in it.

What does the CCPA mean for California urologists?

Business contact data now falls under the CCPA. Companies the law covers must honor deletion requests from California urologists and keep those contacts out of later sends.

Do the urologists mailing lists work for postal campaigns?

Yes. Before delivery, practice addresses go through address standardization checks, so a single file covers both your email sequence and your printed mailers, with no extra data purchase.

How fresh are the records in our urologists mailing lists?

Urology records go through a full recheck every quarter. Doctors who retire or close a practice are removed, and newly licensed urologists join once their license and practice are verified.

Why does one urologist email list quote differ from another?

It depends on how many records you need and which subspecialties and states you pick. You see the number once your sample test is done, so it matches the file you approve.

What format do files come in?

CSV or Excel, delivered within a few business days of sample approval, with column headers that stay the same across repeat orders so repeat imports into your CRM need no remapping.

What gets a urologist to answer a cold email?

Open the email with the condition your product treats, such as kidney stones or BPH, and keep it short. Judge results by replies and meetings, as Apple's privacy features now load emails automatically.

Do you replace urologist email addresses that bounce?

Before delivery, every address is tested to a 97% deliverability standard. If one hard-bounces within the replacement window, a newly verified urologist from the same subspecialty and state takes its place.
