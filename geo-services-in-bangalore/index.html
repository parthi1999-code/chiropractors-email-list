GEO vs SEO

What GEO is and how it differs from SEO

GEO is the work of getting your brand mentioned and cited inside answers that generative AI models write. SEO still matters: it earns rankings and clicks on a results page. GEO covers what happens when a model reads several sources and writes its own summary. You'll also hear the term AEO for this work. The 2 overlap heavily, and our services cover both.

If your pages state facts plainly and independent sources repeat them, a model can use you. If your claims are vague or stand alone, it uses a competitor.

AreaSEOGEO
GoalRank pages and earn clicksGet mentioned and cited in AI answers
Where it showsSearch results pagesChat replies, summaries and AI search results
Core inputsKeywords, links, page speedQuotable facts, entity clarity, structured data, third-party mentions
Main measuresRankings and organic trafficCitations, mentions and share of answer

Good GEO sits on top of good SEO. A site with crawl errors or thin pages won't get cited, so we fix the basics first when they're broken.

Process

How our GEO process works

01

Audit

We run a fixed set of prompts across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude and Google AI Overviews, then record who gets cited and how your brand is described. We also crawl your site for technical blockers. You get a plain-language report, usually within 2 weeks.

02

Prompt map

We turn the audit and your sales notes into a ranked list of questions worth winning. Each question gets an owner page, a target answer and a proof point.

03

Foundation fixes

We repair crawl access, rendering, schema and entity data so machines can read your pages. This step is quick for many sites.

04

Content

We write new pages that open with the direct answer, then add detail, data and sources, and we rework pages that already earn traffic.

05

Mentions

We earn coverage on the sources models read, through outreach, digital PR and accurate listings. Mentions only count when they're real, so we don't buy placements.

06

Review

Each month we rerun the prompt set, compare results to the baseline and adjust the plan.

Steps 3 to 5 overlap in practice. Fixes, content and mention work run in parallel, and each month's review decides where the next hours go.
