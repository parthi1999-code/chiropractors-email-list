Who We Are

Infinyx Global is a new-generation B2B growth company, established in 2022 on a simple idea: growth should not need a patchwork of vendors. One agency for data, another for outreach, a third for SEO and a fourth for hiring means slow handoffs and no shared accountability.

We built Infinyx to remove that friction. Our team combines developers, designers, testers, data specialists, demand-generation marketers, search strategists and recruiters, bde’s who work to the same plan, against the same targets. Whether you need a clean contact list, a pipeline of qualified meetings, visibility in Google and AI answers, or a sharp technical hire from India, you get one partner and one point of accountability.

OUR JOURNEY

Our Story

We started Infinyx in 2022 after seeing the same problem repeatedly. Businesses were buying contact lists that bounced, paying for campaigns that never reached decision-makers, and ranking on search pages while staying invisible in AI-generated answers.

We decided to build a company that treats data quality as the foundation, then layers outreach, search visibility and talent on top. Being a young company means we carry no legacy processes. We build every workflow around how B2B buyers actually research and decide today: through search engines, AI assistants, LinkedIn and email.

Our mission was clear from day one: make growth simpler, more measurable and more trustworthy for B2B teams.
