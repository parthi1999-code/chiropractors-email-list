Questions Buyers Ask Before Ordering a Dentist Email List

What does a dentist email list include, and who uses it?

It’s a file of verified contact details for licensed dentists, with each row tied to 1 named dentist. Sales and hiring teams use it to reach dentists at their practices by email or by post.

Why buy a dentist email address list instead of a dental practice list?

A practice list gives you 1 row per office, often with a shared inbox. A dentist-level file gives you 1 row per licensed dentist, tagged by specialty and inbox type, so each message reaches the person you meant.

How many verified contacts are in the dentists email database?
There are 1.1M+ verified dental contacts in the Infinyx Global file. Before you order, a count against your specialty and state filters shows exactly how many of them match your campaign.
Can a dentist email list be narrowed to one specialty and one territory?
Yes. Pick general dentistry or any of the 12 recognized dental specialties, then narrow by state, ZIP code, practice type or DSO status. Orthodontists in Texas, for example, can be a file of their own.
What does inbox type mean on a dentist record?
It’s a tag on each email in the file. Dentist direct means the address belongs to the dentist, and shared practice inbox means the email lands at a front-desk address such as office@.
Is it possible to pull only owner dentists, or only DSO dentists, from dentist email lists?
Yes. Records carry ownership role and DSO tags, so you can pull owner dentists at independent practices and dentists at DSO-affiliated offices as 2 separate files with their own messages.
Can dentists email lists be split by practice size?
Yes. Each record carries the practice’s headcount and DSO status, so you can separate solo offices from larger groups and write to each in its own terms.
What does CAN-SPAM require when I email dentists from a bought file?
CAN-SPAM lets you email dentists at their practices without prior consent, as long as each message carries your postal address, an ad disclosure, truthful headers, an honest subject line and a working opt-out. For dentists abroad, check GDPR or CASL first.
Does HIPAA apply to a dentist mailing list with no patient data?
No. HIPAA is about the patient health information that providers and health plans keep. A dentist mailing list carries practice contact details and nothing from patient charts, so the privacy rule has nothing in it to cover.
Can California dentists ask to be removed from my campaigns?
Yes, if your business is covered by the CCPA. The law now covers business contact data too, so a California dentist can ask you to delete their details from your files.
Do the dentists mailing lists include postal addresses for mail drops?
Yes. Each record carries a practice mailing address processed through address standardization, so the same file runs your email campaign and your postcard drop without a second purchase.
How do dentists mailing lists stay current when dentists retire or move?
We re-verify the whole file every quarter. Retired dentists and closed practices come out, and dentists newly licensed in the latest registry release go in, with their practice links checked.
How is a dentist email list order quoted?
It’s based on your requirements, including the number of records, specialties, states and delivery format. We share it once you’ve reviewed your sample and settled the final filters, so the number reflects the exact file you’ll receive.
Which file formats do you deliver, and how soon after sample approval?
You get CSV or Excel, with clean column headers your CRM or email platform can read without reformatting, within a few business days of approving your sample, in the same layout every time you reorder.
What gets a busy dentist to open and reply to a cold email?
Keep the email short and tied to a procedure or practice problem the dentist already deals with. Count replies and booked demos as your results, since Apple’s Mail Privacy Protection makes open rates look higher than they are.
What happens when an address in the dentist database bounces?
Before delivery, each address in the dentist email database must clear a 97% deliverability check. A hard bounce reported within the replacement window is replaced by a freshly checked dentist from the same segment of your dentist email address list.
