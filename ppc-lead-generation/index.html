Overview

PPC lead generation can create immediate visibility for relevant searches, but clicks alone are not the objective. The campaign needs the right keywords, audience, message, landing page, tracking, and qualification path. Infinyx builds paid search campaigns around lead quality and measurable conversion actions rather than traffic volume alone.

What we do

Build PPC Around Leads, Not Just Clicks

Common problems

PPC problems often come from weak targeting, poor landing page alignment, or measurement gaps rather than the platform itself.

Broad or Irrelevant Keywords

Weak Negative Keyword Control

Ad and Landing Page Mismatch

The page does not continue the intent or promise of the ad.

Poor Conversion Tracking

Campaign decisions are made without reliable lead data.

Low Lead Quality

Campaigns generate form submissions that do not match sales criteria.

No Sales Feedback Loop

Marketing optimizes for platform conversions without understanding downstream lead quality.
