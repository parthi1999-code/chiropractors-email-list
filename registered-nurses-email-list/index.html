Why a nurse-only list

Why Healthcare Marketers Need a Registered Nurses Email List

Registered nurses are a large audience spread across thousands of facilities. The US had about 3.47 million registered nurse jobs in 2025, and the Bureau of Labor Statistics projects 6% growth from 2025 to 2035. Many nurses work 12-hour shifts and read email once they're off the floor, so an inbox message waits until they have time for it.

A verified registered nurse email database fixes 2 problems that weaken healthcare campaigns:

Wrong audience

Broad healthcare lists mix nurses with physicians and office staff, so messages reach people who can't act on them

Bad data

Stale addresses bounce, and a high bounce rate damages your sender reputation on every campaign that follows

A list built only for registered nurses puts each message in front of a licensed nurse in the specialty and region you chose. Nurse-only data tends to produce fewer bounces and more relevant replies than broad healthcare files.

Compliance

Compliance for US and International Campaigns

The list is built for lawful B2B outreach, and each file ships with the suppression data you need to stay compliant.

In the US, the CAN-SPAM Act governs commercial email and makes no exception for B2B messages. The Act doesn't require prior consent, and penalties reach up to $53,088 for each email that breaks its rules. Every message must meet the requirements set out in the FTC compliance guide:

Accurate sender details and subject lines that match the content
A clear and visible disclosure that the message is an ad
A valid physical postal address for your business
A working opt-out, with requests honored within 10 business days
EU and UK

In the EU and UK, GDPR governs the personal data, and national ePrivacy laws set the email rules, which differ by country. The UK allows email to corporate addresses without prior consent if you identify yourself and offer an opt-out, while Germany requires consent even for B2B email.

Canada and HIPAA

Canada's anti-spam law, CASL, requires express or implied consent, so confirm your basis for contact before emailing Canadian nurses. HIPAA doesn't apply to the list itself, since it governs patient health information and a nurse's work email and job title aren't patient data.
