The basics

What's Technographic Data?

Technographic data is a record of the technology a company uses, from the software it pays for to the infrastructure it runs on. It shows which products a company has installed and the category each one belongs to. That tells you whether an account can use what you sell. Sales and marketing teams use it to pick accounts and shape what they say to them.

A technographics data email list adds the people behind those tools, with verified contacts for the buyers and managers of each one. A record might show that a 400-person logistics company runs Salesforce as its CRM and hosts its apps on AWS. The IT director's verified email sits in the same row. This list is the contact layer of our technographic data services.

Two kinds of data

Technographic Data vs. Firmographic Data

Technographic data shows what a company uses, and firmographic data shows what kind of company it is. Good targeting needs both. A cybersecurity vendor, for example, uses firmographics to find mid-size banks and technographics to find the ones still running on-premises servers. Every record in our list carries both sets of fields.

How we build it

How Our Technographic Database Is Built and Kept Current

Every record passes through 5 stages before it reaches your list. Each stage removes a different kind of error, from wrong tools to outdated contacts.

01

Track the technology

We log which of our 27,000+ tracked technologies each company runs, across software, cloud, infrastructure and web tools. That builds a stack profile for every company in the database.

02

Match it to the company

Each stack profile links to company details such as industry, employee count, revenue and location. Your list can then filter by company fit and by technology at the same time.

03

Add the decision-makers

We attach named contacts with job titles for the people who buy and manage each technology. Those titles let you separate technical evaluators from the budget holders.

04

Verify every record

Our team checks each record and email address before it goes into a list. Records that fail the check stay out of your file, which keeps data accuracy at 95%.

05

Refresh every 90 days

We refresh the full database every 90 days, so stale records don't sit in your file. Each refresh catches tool switches and job changes, and your list reflects what companies run today.

Accuracy and compliance

Data Accuracy and Sending Compliance

Accuracy comes from checks at every stage of the build. Hard bounces are replaced with fresh records within 30 days, and files move to you over encrypted SFTP. Your sample file shows where each record came from and when it was last verified. Every file is checked against the filters you approved before it leaves our team.

Compliance depends on how you send. Contacts in the EU fall under the GDPR, so confirm your lawful basis before you email them. California residents have rights under the CCPA, including the right to ask you to delete their data. The CAN-SPAM Act covers business email in the US too. Check that every campaign does these 4 things:

Include your valid physical postal address in every message.
Give a clear opt-out and honor each request within 10 business days.
Keep sender details accurate and match subject lines to the content.
Disclose clearly and visibly that the message is an advertisement.
